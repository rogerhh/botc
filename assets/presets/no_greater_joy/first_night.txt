Dusk
Start the Night Phase.

Minion Info
Wake all Minions: Show the THIS IS THE DEMON token. Point to the Demon. Show the THESE ARE YOUR MINIONS token. Point to the other Minions.

Demon Info
Wake the Demon: Show the THESE ARE YOUR MINIONS token. Point to all Minions. Show the THESE CHARACTERS ARE NOT IN PLAY token. Show 3 not-in-play good character tokens.

Investigator
Show the Minion character token. Point to both the MINION and WRONG players.

Empath
Give a finger signal.

Clockmaker
Give a finger signal.

Chambermaid
The Chambermaid chooses 2 living players. Give a finger signal.

Dawn
Wait for a few seconds. End the Night Phase.
